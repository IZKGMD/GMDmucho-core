The platform

A control layer around the server you already own.

MuchoGDPS is designed to make the infrastructure boring: deploy the stack, keep the data on your VPS, and build your GDPS on top of MuchoCore.

⚡

Deploy the whole stack, not a checklist

The installer handles the boring pieces around your GDPS so you can get to the part that matters: your server, your content, and your community.

DockerMariaDBCaddyMuchoCoreHealth checks
🧩

Database adapters

Migration logic is built around adapters, so different GDPS schemas can be handled without forcing every server into one database layout.

🛡️

Your infrastructure stays yours

The long-term architecture uses a lightweight agent and short-lived credentials instead of keeping your VPS root password in the website.

Designed for the messy reality of GDPS databases.

Some servers share a schema. Some do not. MuchoCore can grow through adapters instead of assuming that every backup looks the same.

How it works

From an empty VPS to a working GDPS.

The flow is intentionally simple now, with room for a proper control plane later.

01

Connect

Provide the VPS details needed for the initial deployment or use a short-lived deployment token.

02

Deploy

The installer prepares the environment and brings up MuchoCore with the required services.

03

Configure

Set the domain, compatibility profile, database path, and the pieces that make your GDPS yours.

04

Manage

Move toward one control plane for nodes, updates, backups, migrations, and operational logs.

Bring your own VPS

Own the machine. Let MuchoGDPS handle the boring parts.

Start with a VPS you control. MuchoCore runs on it, your data stays there, and the future node agent can provide ongoing management without turning your server credentials into a permanent dependency.
