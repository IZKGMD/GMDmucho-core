Why MuchoGDPS

Hosting without the hosting headache.

You keep control of the machine. MuchoGDPS focuses on deployment, management, and the GDPS layer instead of locking you into our infrastructure.

⚡

One-click deployment

Connect a VPS and automate the installation path instead of manually assembling PHP, MariaDB, Docker, and the reverse proxy.

🧩

Built for different databases

MuchoCore is being designed around adapters so migration logic does not assume every GDPS uses the same SQL schema.

🛡️

You keep the server

Your compute stays on your VPS. Credentials and long-lived root access should not be stored by the public website.

How it works
